Czech Speaker Guidance

In London or US boardroom culture, opening with pure disagreement sounds confrontational. Always acknowledge the CFO's fiduciary duty before presenting counter-data.

Executive Speech Rules
  • Acknowledge first: "That is a fair pushback..."
  • Avoid literal Czech translations like "We must solve" or "You must understand".
  • Anchor arguments with financial collocations: mitigate risk, unit economics, payback runway.
